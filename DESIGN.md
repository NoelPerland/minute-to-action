# Minute to Action design

- Role: turn rough notes into editable decisions, actions and open questions, then keep a working journal.
- Voice: precise and honest. Unknown owners and dates remain unknown; AI drafts remain reviewable.
- Type: Newsreader for document/editorial titles, DM Sans for controls, IBM Plex Mono for actual metadata.
- Color: paper and navy with restrained lime cues. The meeting document and action board remain distinct.
- Layout: real note-to-draft example on the public page; task and document content lead inside the workspace.
- Motion: transformation and explicit state changes only; reduced motion remains readable.
